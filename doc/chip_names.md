# CHIP NAMES AND MEANINGS

## W65C02

Central Processing Unit (CPU)

The orchestrator, control center, and brain.

### Sophronia

"Self-controlled, prudent, and wise." A perfect fit for a CPU that spends its life strictly fetching, decoding, and executing instructions under tight timing controls without ever losing discipline.

## W65C22

Versatile Interface Adapter (VIA)

The ultimate multitasker. Handles parallel I/O, background timers, and data shifting.

### Millicent

"Strong in work" or "industrious." The VIA is the ultimate workplace laborer of the system, juggling multiple input/output pins, generating interrupts, and clocking pulses behind the scenes.

## W65C51

Asynchronous Communications Interface Adapter (ACIA)

The UART serial controller that speaks to external terminals.

### Eulalia

"Sweetly speaking" or "well-spoken." An eccentric Victorian favorite that perfectly describes a communications chip responsible for cleanly articulating data bits down a serial line.

## MAX232

Dual RS-232 Driver/Receiver

A voltage level shifter. It translates 5V TTL logic up to ±12V serial signals and back.

### Iris

"The rainbow goddess / messenger who bridges worlds." Just as the mythological Iris bridged heaven and earth, this chip acts as a literal bridge between two entirely different voltage domains, translating foreign electrical signals so they can understand each other.
